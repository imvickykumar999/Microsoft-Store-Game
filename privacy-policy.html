Legal / Data Practices

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Warzone3D ("the Client") handles information.

Information the Client Uses

The Client allows you to enter:

  • A player username
  • A game server address
  • A game server port

During gameplay, the Client sends game data to the selected server, including:

  • Your username
  • Your assigned player ID
  • Player position and rotation
  • Health status
  • Shooting, damage, and respawn events

The Client does not require an account, password, email address, payment information, or personal profile.

Network Information

When connecting to a game server, your IP address may be visible to and processed by:

  • The game server operator
  • Network providers
  • Any hosting, tunneling, or connectivity service used by that server

The Client may inspect local network interfaces to identify available local, Tailscale, and other network addresses for server connection suggestions. This information is used locally to populate the server-selection list and is not separately sent to the developer.

Game traffic is sent using a TCP connection to the server selected by the user. Do not use a personal name or other sensitive information as your username. Server operators may independently retain connection logs, usernames, gameplay data, and IP addresses according to their own privacy policies.

Information We Collect

The Client does not directly collect or transmit analytics, advertising identifiers, cookies, browsing history, contacts, files, precise location, microphone recordings, camera data, or payment information.

The Client does not create a persistent user account or maintain a developer-controlled online profile.

How Information Is Used

Information exchanged with the selected game server is used to:

  • Connect you to a multiplayer match
  • Display your player identity to other players
  • Synchronize player movement and game state
  • Process shooting, damage, health, and respawn events
  • Diagnose connection failures when displayed locally by the Client

Data Storage and Retention

The Client does not intentionally store player information after it closes. The selected game server may temporarily or permanently retain connection and gameplay information. Contact the relevant server operator to request information about their retention and deletion practices.

Children's Privacy

The Client is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Client. Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information may contact us using the address below.

Third-Party Services

The Client may connect to a game server operated by the developer or by another party. Those servers and any associated hosting, tunneling, or network services may process information independently. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled by the Client. However, network communications depend on the selected server and network infrastructure. No internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Client's data practices change. The updated policy will include a revised effective date.
